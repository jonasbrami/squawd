### Root causes found and fixed along the way (~30 gate runs, in order)

1. PX4 Land-mode arming lock (hold→arm→takeoff everywhere) + cross-world
   EKF2_MAG_DECL poisoning (factory-state wipe at launch).
2. gz topic derived from sensor *name* not type (RANGE_TOPIC).
3. Blob thresholds tuned to the real rendered orange (was tuned to SDF ideal).
4. mov_1 base floats at z=0.6 (support plane; visible over its shadow).
5. Aim/travel-heading left the FOV off the mover (frame-cadence live-bearing
   aim + bearing association gate).
6. Recorder clock skew PX4-hrt vs gz-time (EMA-tracked offset; was starving
   pose_at/attitude_at after ~0.1% drift × hours).
7. Offboard priming NO_SETPOINT_SET retries.
8. Soft-start ref blending (PX4 P-term pitch spikes at track start).
9. **The headline: pitch sign bug** (`dep = ay − pitch`, was `ay + pitch`) —
   every geom projection during any maneuver was corrupted (fable round 1).
10. support_z=0.6 for the mover class; frame-edge clip guard; honest attitude
    fallback; bearing fallback for NIS-rejected geom; graveyard rebind
    predicts to match time.
11. Trajectory shaper (v = v_target + Kp·err, clamped, slew-limited,
    integrated ref) — MAVSDK ff is NOT a constraint (codex P1).
12. MPC_TILTMAX_AIR=12°, MPC_XY_VEL_MAX=6 at connect (offboard bypasses PX4
    jerk limits, issue #18033).
13. Detector 10 Hz conf 0.2; EKF warm-up hover; aim-lead; feedforward clamp.
14. Name-churn adoption (pursuit follows the OBJECT across ephemeral ids,
    predicted-forward gate, unique match).
15. **mover z drift** (sim mover commanded vz=0; z wandered ±1 m ≈ 4–5 m
    projection error — fable round 2 headline): proportional vz hold.
16. COASTING-aware pursuit (no closing on prediction); measured-bearing yaw;
    lost wall-clock backstop; visibility guard (7 m ref floor); gap-dependent
    altitude; bbox angular-height range channel (σ≈0.8 m second cue).

### What still kills runs (honest, not theories)

- Start-geometry variance: the mover's orbit phase at track start; ~1/3 of
  starts are immediately favorable, the rest blind the first seconds.
- The estimate's orbit-phase error oscillation (±4–7 m) occasionally parks
  the truth gap >15 m long enough to break the 45 s contiguity, or parks the
  mover at a frame edge (bearing-only starvation → LOST by design).
